Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-11-21

The short version

Saybig doesn't collect any data. There is no account, no analytics, no ads and no third-party code. Your voice is recognized on your iPhone and is never uploaded or saved as a recording.

What stays on your iPhone

Saybig keeps a few things on your iPhone only: your settings (text size, colors, recognition language, Face to Face Mode, Save History) and your History of sentences, which you can turn off or clear any time in the app. They are never sent anywhere, and we can't see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Microphone and speech recognition

When you tap the microphone button for the first time, iOS asks for permission to use the microphone. Audio is processed in memory on your iPhone to turn it into text, and is not written to a file. The only time Saybig goes online is the first time you use a language: iOS downloads that language's speech recognition data. That download is handled by Apple's system, not by Saybig.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We don't receive your payment details or your Apple account information.

Children

Saybig doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
