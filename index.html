Saybig

Speech to Big Text for iPhone.

Talk to your iPhone and Saybig turns your words into huge text on the screen, so the person across from you can simply read it. The person reading never has to touch the phone.

  • Talk, and they read. Your words appear as big, bold text a moment after you say them. Stand the phone up, or turn it around to show them.
  • Type when talking won't do. Type a sentence and show it in the same big text. This keeps working after the free trial.
  • Stays on your phone. Your voice is recognized on the iPhone itself. It is not uploaded and not saved as a recording. No account, no ads, no data collection.
  • Made for iPhone Duo too. Half-open, it stands on the table like a caption card: big text facing them, controls facing you.

Good for talking with grandparents, at a clinic counter or pharmacy, with a caregiver, or anywhere it is too noisy to be heard. Speech recognition understands Mandarin (Taiwan) and English. It does not understand Taiwanese Hokkien, and like any speech recognition it will sometimes get a word wrong, so check the small preview on your side.
