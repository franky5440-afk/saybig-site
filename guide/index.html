User guide

For Saybig 1.0 on iPhone (iOS 26 or later).

How it works

The screen has two parts. The big text area at the top is for the other person. The control area at the bottom, about 30% of the screen, is for you: the microphone button, a small preview of the latest sentence, and four small buttons (Keyboard, Face-to-face, History, Settings), plus Clear next to the preview.

First time

  1. Open Saybig. The first screen reads “Try Saybig free for 7 days”.
  2. Tap Start 7-Day Free Trial. It costs nothing. (Or tap Buy Full Version to unlock it right away.)
  3. You land on the main screen. It says “Tap the microphone and start talking”.

Talk and show

  1. Tap the microphone button. The first time, iPhone asks to use the microphone. Tap Allow.
  2. The first time you use a language, iPhone downloads its speech recognition data once. You will see “Downloading speech recognition resources (one time only)” with progress. This is the only time Saybig goes online, and iOS does the download. If it fails, tap Try Again.
  3. Talk. Your words appear in the big text area. Text that may still change is a little see-through; once it settles it turns solid.
  4. When you pause briefly, or finish a sentence, your next words start on a new line. Older lines dim, and the newest one stays bright.
  5. Tap the microphone button again to pause. Tap it once more to carry on.

The small preview in the control area shows your latest sentence so you can check what the other person sees. If you say nothing for 3 minutes, Saybig pauses itself. If you leave the app or a call comes in, it stops listening, and you tap the microphone again to resume. While it listens, the screen does not auto-lock.

Scrolled back to read an earlier sentence? Tap Jump to latest, or just leave it for 5 seconds and it jumps back when new words arrive. Tap Clear to wipe the big text area and start a new conversation. Your History is not affected.

Face to face

When the other person sits across from you, the big text area is turned upside down so it reads correctly from their side. This is on by default. Tap Face-to-face to turn it off, for example when you hand the phone over, or when you sit side by side. You can also set it in Settings.

Type instead of talking

  1. Tap Keyboard. A text box appears and the keyboard comes up. Listening pauses.
  2. Type your sentence and tap Show. It appears in the big text area. The box clears and the keyboard stays, so you can keep typing.
  3. Tap Close Keyboard to go back to the controls.

Typing to big text keeps working after the free trial ends.

History

Tap History to see past sentences, grouped by day, newest first. Typed sentences have a small keyboard icon.

  • Tap a sentence to show it full screen again, in your current text size and colors. Tap once more, or tap Close, to come back.
  • Swipe left on a sentence to delete it. Tap Clear All (and confirm) to delete everything.
  • History is stored only on your iPhone. Turn off Save History in Settings and new sentences are no longer saved.

History keeps working after the free trial ends.

Settings

  • Full Version: shows your trial days left, or “Unlocked”, plus Buy and Restore Purchases.
  • Text Size: five steps, from “10 characters per line” to “5 characters per line”. The default is 7. Fewer characters per line means bigger text.
  • Colors: White on black (default) or Black on white. This applies to the big text area only.
  • Language (recognition language): Chinese (Taiwan) or English (US). By default it follows your iPhone language. It is separate from the language of the app's buttons.
  • Face to Face: on or off (see above).
  • Save History: on or off. On by default.
  • About: version, this guide, the privacy policy.

Free trial and the full version

  • Saybig is a free download with a 7-day free trial. During the trial everything works: talk to big text, type to big text, and History.
  • After 7 days, typing to big text and looking at History still work. Talk to big text needs the full version, a one-time US$2.99 purchase. There is no subscription and no ads.
  • When the trial has ended and you tap the microphone, a screen explains this. Tap Buy Full Version to unlock, Restore Purchases if you bought before, or Not Now to go back.

iPhone Duo

On iPhone Duo (iOS 27.1 or later), Saybig adapts to how the phone is folded. Other iPhones are not affected.

  • Caption card (half-open, held upright): stand it on the table like a small laptop. The upright upper half faces the other person and shows the big text (never upside down). The flat lower half has your controls. With Face to Face Mode on, the controls are turned around so you can read them from your side of the table. Buttons stay clear of the fold.
  • Fully open, upright: the big text area fills the screen, so more sentences fit at once, with a slim control strip at the bottom.
  • Closed: the same layout as a regular iPhone.
  • Inner screen held sideways: big text on the left, controls and a small list of this conversation on the right. Tap a sentence in the list to jump to it.

Switching postures does not interrupt listening and does not lose your text or a half-typed sentence.

Good to know

  • Speech recognition is not perfect. You may see wrong words that sound alike. Check the small preview, and use Keyboard when something important must be exact.
  • Recognition understands Mandarin (Taiwan) and English. Taiwanese Hokkien is not supported, and neither are Cantonese or Japanese. If you mix languages in one sentence, the part in the other language will not come out right.
  • Noisy rooms and a phone far from your mouth make recognition worse. Hold it close.
  • Saybig shows whatever the microphone hears. If a TV, a video or other people are talking nearby, their words may appear while you pause.
  • Saybig does not translate, does not record audio, does not export anything, and does not sync to iCloud.
