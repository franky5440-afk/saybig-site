Common questions

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Saybig and tap Settings, then Full Version, then Restore Purchases. On the free trial screen and the “Your free trial has ended” screen, Restore Purchases is there too. After changing phones or reinstalling, sign in with the same Apple account you bought with, then tap Restore Purchases.

What happens when the free trial ends?

Saybig still opens. Typing to big text, History and Settings keep working. Talk to big text needs the full version, a one-time US$2.99 purchase with no subscription. When you tap the microphone after the trial, a screen shows the options: Buy Full Version, Restore Purchases or Not Now.

The microphone button does nothing, or says it needs permission

You may have said No when iPhone asked about the microphone. Tap Open Settings on the screen, or open the iPhone Settings app, tap Saybig, and turn Microphone on. Typing keeps working in the meantime.

It says “Downloading speech recognition resources”

The first time you use a language, iOS downloads its recognition data once, so your iPhone needs an internet connection for that. If it fails, tap Try Again. After that, Saybig works without a connection.

It doesn't recognize my Taiwanese Hokkien

Saybig understands Mandarin (Taiwan) and English only. Taiwanese Hokkien, Cantonese and Japanese are not supported. Mixed speech will come out wrong in the other-language parts. You can type those words with Keyboard.

Some words are wrong

Speech recognition can pick a word that sounds the same but is wrong. Speak a little slower, hold the phone closer, and check the small preview. For anything that must be exact, use Keyboard.

Is my voice recorded or uploaded?

No. Your voice is recognized on the iPhone, never uploaded and never saved as an audio file. See the privacy policy.

Can I use it on iPad or Apple Watch?

No. Saybig is for iPhone only.

How do I delete what I've said?

Open History, swipe left to delete one sentence, or tap Clear All. To stop saving, turn off Save History in Settings. Deleting the app removes everything.
